# Carom

Two paddles. Two obstacles. A thousand possible angles. Carom is a fast paddle
duel where a straight shot is only the beginning. Take on the computer in Solo,
or bring a friend for a local Versus match.

The first serve gives you a moment to find your footing. Then the rally begins:
read the bounce, meet the ball, and send it back faster. Slide your paddle at the
moment of contact to bend its flight. A well timed sweep can turn an ordinary
return into a shot that slips past your rival.

The amber bars make the middle of the court a playground for bank shots. Aim
around them, ricochet off them, or let a curling ball find an unexpected route.
Every return asks you to balance a safe contact against a sharper angle.

Race to eleven, with a two point lead to settle the match. At ten all, every
rally matters. Pause for a breath, run it back, and find a new angle.
